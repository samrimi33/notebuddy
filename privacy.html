Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: NoteBuddy is a private notebook. Your notes, diary, lists, passwords and photos are stored on your phone. If you turn on cloud sync, they are encrypted on your phone before they are uploaded, so we can't read them. There are no ads, no trackers and no selling of data.

Who we are

NoteBuddy is made by Shivam Mishra. Contact: samrimi33@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create — notes, diary pages, photos, voice notes, tasks, grocery lists, expenses, health logs, the password vault and settings — is saved in the app's private storage on your device. Locked notes and the vault are encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure keystore. Backups you export are encrypted with a password you choose and saved wherever you decide.

Google Sign-In

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google (scopes: openid, email, profile). We use them only to create your NoteBuddy account and show who is signed in. We never get your Google password and we don't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Cloud sync (optional)

If you turn on sync, your data is encrypted on your phone before upload and stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. Only your devices hold the key, so neither we nor Supabase can read your notes. We can see basic account information (your email, when you signed up and how much space your encrypted data uses) to run the service.

Features that use services on your phone

  • Document scanning and reading text from photos run on your phone (Google ML Kit, on-device). Images are not uploaded.
  • Dictation uses your phone's speech-recognition service. When your phone has an offline speech model it runs on the device; otherwise your phone's provider (usually Google) processes the audio under its own privacy policy. NoteBuddy shows which one is in use.
  • Read aloud uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech.
  • AI assistant (optional, off by default): only when you use it, the text you choose is sent to Anthropic's Claude API using an API key you provide. It is never used automatically and locked notes are never sent.

Update check

About twice a day NoteBuddy downloads a small file (version.json) from our GitHub page to see if a newer version exists. Nothing about you or your notes is sent. Like any website, GitHub can see your phone's IP address when the file is downloaded.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders and home-screen widgets are created on your phone. In privacy mode, notifications and widgets hide what your notes say.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Password files you import are read on your phone and never uploaded.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone is deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall NoteBuddy. To delete your account and any synced (encrypted) data, use “Delete account” in the app's settings once sync is available, or email samrimi33@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

NoteBuddy is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention important changes in the app.
