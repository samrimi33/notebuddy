Installing NoteBuddy

Why Google Play Protect may warn you, what it means, and how to install safely.

Why does Play Protect warn about or block NoteBuddy?

NoteBuddy is downloaded from GitHub, not the Google Play Store. Play Protect checks every app installed from outside Play. It treats an app it hasn't seen before more cautiously, especially a new app from a small developer. It is stricter again when the app asks for powerful permissions. NoteBuddy asks for two:

  • Reading SMS: used only for the optional "Payments from SMS" feature, which turns bank and wallet messages into expenses on your phone.
  • Autofill service: used only if you turn on password autofill from your vault.

Apps that read SMS or fill in passwords are also what scam apps ask for. Play Protect can't tell an honest new app from a dishonest one, so it may warn you or block the install. The warning means "Google doesn't know this app yet". It does not mean a virus was found.

If the message names a specific threat, or says the app "contains malware" or was "removed", don't install it. Delete the file and email samrimi33@gmail.com. You may have downloaded a copy from somewhere other than our official page.

Before you install: make sure it's the real app

  1. Download only from the official GitHub releases page, never from another website or a forwarded file.
  2. Optional check for advanced users: every official NoteBuddy APK is signed with the same certificate.
    Signing certificate SHA-256
    6C:D4:05:42:E1:23:FF:39:0B:8B:D7:F0:1E:77:9E:87:AD:80:76:CA:55:B4:E9:A5:24:50:C0:AD:DA:BA:36:D7
    You can check it with apksigner verify --print-certs NoteBuddy.apk, or with an app such as "App Manager". Each release's notes also list the file's SHA-256 checksum.

How to install

  1. Open NoteBuddy.apk from your downloads and tap Install. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  2. If you see "Unsafe app blocked" or "Play Protect warning", tap More details (or the small arrow), then Install anyway.
  3. If there's no "Install anyway" button:
    1. Open the Play Store → tap your profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙ settings.
    2. Turn off Scan apps with Play Protect for now and install NoteBuddy.
    3. Turn Play Protect back on straight away. It keeps protecting you from other apps.
  4. If Play Protect later suggests removing NoteBuddy, you can choose to keep it. Your data stays on your phone either way.

Turning on "Payments from SMS" (Android 13 and newer)

For apps installed from outside Play, Android 13+ hides the SMS permission behind a "Restricted setting". To use the feature:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → NoteBuddy.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, then confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  3. Go back to NoteBuddy and turn on Payments from SMS again.

This step is optional. Everything else in NoteBuddy works without SMS access.

What we're doing about it

  • Google Play release: apps installed from Play aren't flagged this way. The Play version leaves out SMS reading, which Google allows only after a separate review.
  • Asking Google to review the app so Play Protect recognises it.
  • Developer verification: registering NoteBuddy under Google's Android developer verification, so Android knows who made it.

What NoteBuddy does with those permissions

SMS messages are read only on your phone. Only the amount, date and payee are kept, and message text is never saved or sent anywhere.
