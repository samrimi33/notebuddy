## ✨ What's inside

| | Features |
|---|---|
| 📝 **Notes** | Checklists, folders, tags, linked notes with a note map, scanning, dictation, read aloud, flashcards and version history |
| 📖 **Diary** | Lined notebook pages, photo memories, postcards, time capsules, voice diary and weekly reflection. Read it as a real book with page turns, or save it as a PDF to print |
| ✅ **Plan** | Tasks, habits, reminders, timetable, countdowns, meal planner, packing lists and event planner |
| 💸 **Money** | Expenses and income, budgets, recurring bills, split bills, debts, savings jars, warranties and monthly PDF reports |
| 💧 **Health** | Water, sleep and period tracking, medicine reminders, gratitude jar and birthdays |
| 🔐 **Vault** | Passwords, cards, Wi-Fi and keys encrypted with AES-256. Import from Chrome, Bitwarden and more, generate 2FA codes and autofill in other apps |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 **Shared Lists** | Grocery lists the whole family can edit live, with end-to-end encryption |
| ☁️ **Backup** | Encrypted daily backups to your own Google Drive. Restore your data on a new phone |
| 🐧 **Buddy** | A cute penguin friend with games, badges and festival outfits |
| 📱 **Widgets** | Today, grocery, habits, timetable, countdown, search and more on your home screen |

## 🔒 Private by Design

Your personal data should stay personal.

NoteBuddy is built with privacy in mind.

- 🔐 **Local Storage:** Your notes live on your phone, not on a server.
- 🛡️ **AES-256 Encryption:** Locked notes and vault data are encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware.
- 🔑 **App Lock:** Protect the app with a PIN, password or fingerprint.
- ☁️ **Encrypted Backups:** Export your data with encryption using a password you choose.
- 🚫 **No Ads:** Enjoy the app without advertisements.
- 👀 **No Tracking:** No analytics or selling your personal data.
- 📱 **Optional Google Sign-In:** Use the app without signing into Google.

**Your notes. Your data. Your control.**

## 🔄 App Updates

NoteBuddy includes an update checker.

To check for updates:

1. Open **NoteBuddy**.
2. Go to **Settings → About**.
3. Check for the latest available version.
4. Download and install the update.

**Your data stays safe.**

Normal updates install over the existing version without deleting your notes, diary entries, passwords or other saved data.

### ⚠️ Updating from Version 1.0.x?

Some older versions may require a one-time reinstall.

If Android reports that the new version is incompatible:

1. Open the old NoteBuddy app.
2. Go to **Settings → Import & Export**.
3. Select **Export Everything**.
4. Save your backup somewhere safe.
5. Uninstall the old app.
6. Download and install the latest version.
7. Restore your backup.

**Important:** Do not uninstall the old app before exporting your data.
